There is no single "Bogleheadian World Fund" because **your country of residence dictates which funds you can legally buy and which are tax-efficient.**

The Boglehead philosophy dictates: **Low Cost (TER < 0.20%), Broad Diversification (Developed + Emerging, Large + Mid + Small Cap), Physical Replication, Accumulating (usually), and UCITS-compliant (for non-US investors).**

Here is the breakdown by jurisdiction/domicile.

---

### 2. If you are a European / UK / Ex-US Investor (Buying Irish-Domiciled UCITS ETFs)
*You **must** generally use Irish-domiciled UCITS ETFs to avoid US Estate Tax (up to 40% on assets >$60k) and to reclaim US Withholding Tax (WHT) via the US-Ireland Tax Treaty (15% vs 30%).*
