"Auld Lang Syne" is a traditional Scottish song that is often sung at New Year's Eve celebrations. The phrase "auld lang syne" means "old long since," or "times long past." It is a song about remembering and celebrating old friendships and the good times that have been shared in the past. The song is typically sung while holding hands in a circle and reflecting on the past year. The lyrics of the song express the sentiment that, even though time may pass and people may drift apart, the memories of their friendship will always remain.
