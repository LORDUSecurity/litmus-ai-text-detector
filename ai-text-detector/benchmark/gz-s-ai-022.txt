According to the United States Census Bureau, as of 2019, there are approximately 60.6 million people in the United States who speak Spanish at home. This represents about 18.5% of the total population of the United States. The number of Spanish speakers in the United States has been steadily increasing in recent decades due to immigration from Latin America and the high birth rate among Hispanic populations. Spanish is the second most commonly spoken language in the United States, after English.
