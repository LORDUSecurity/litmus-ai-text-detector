Bogleheadian World ETFs or index funds are investment products that track the performance of a particular market index, such as the S&P 500 or the MSCI World Index. They are named after John Bogle, the founder of Vanguard and a pioneer of index investing. These ETFs or index funds are offered by various asset management firms and can be purchased through brokerage firms or online trading platforms. They are typically low-cost and diversified investment options that aim to provide investors with broad exposure to the global stock market.
